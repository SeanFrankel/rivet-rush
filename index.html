Field manual Movement, attacks & upgrades

Move & evade
A/D steers. Tap W for a short hop, hold for height, or jump again in the air. Shift dashes. Jump during a clean dash to carry speed. S drops through decks, fast-falls, or slides on the ground at speed. Jump out of a slide to keep moving.

Attack & protect
Hold J for assisted fire or aim with the left mouse button. Hold fire and dash for a shoulder strike. K or right-click pulses on the ground or dives from the air. The Warden also shields nearby allies.

Farm & siege
Finish crawlers for bonus scrap and collect the glowing salvage. Buy up to six mods. Use 1 for damage, 2 for health and faster dashes, or 3 for stronger abilities. The next price is on your rig. Escort every third wave’s Breaker, destroy the sentry and attack the reactor. Fall back to your base for repairs. G marks a rally and H a retreat at your cursor, or at your pilot with the cursor outside the arena. Only your crew sees these signals; bots keep their own plans.
